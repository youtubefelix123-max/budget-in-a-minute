How Etsy fees work in 2026

FeeRate
Listing fee$0.20 per listing
Transaction fee6.5% of price + shipping
Payment processing (US)3% + $0.25 per order
Offsite Ads fee12–15% of price + shipping

Every Etsy sale pays the $0.20 listing fee and the 6.5% transaction fee. Payment processing depends on your country — 3% + $0.25 is the US rate. The Offsite Ads fee only applies if the sale came from an Etsy ad: 15% for shops under $10,000 in annual sales, 12% for shops over $10,000. Listings last four months and renew for $0.20.

Common questions

How much does Etsy take from a sale?For a US shop, about 9.5% + $0.45 on every order, before the listing fee — 6.5% transaction plus 3% + $0.25 payment processing, charged on the price plus shipping.

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee and the 3% payment processing fee are both charged on the item price plus the shipping you charge the buyer. Free shipping means those fees only apply to the item price.

What is the Offsite Ads fee?Etsy advertises your listings on Google, Facebook, and other sites. If one of those ads leads to a sale within 30 days, Etsy takes an extra 12% or 15%. Shops under $10,000 in sales can opt out; shops over $10,000 cannot.

Rates are US Etsy rates as of November 2026, checked against Etsy's Fees & Payments Policy. Payment processing rates and regulatory fees vary by country. Sales tax is not included — Etsy collects and remits it separately in most places.
